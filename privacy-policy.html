Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

Spero LLC FZ ("Spero", "we", "us" or "our") provides fractional CFO and financial advisory services to clients in the United Arab Emirates and worldwide. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose and protect information when you visit our website, contact us, or engage our services.

1. Information We Collect

We collect information in the following ways:

  • Information you provide directly: your name, email address, phone number, company name and any details you share when you book a consultation, send an enquiry, or engage us as a client.
  • Client and financial information: where you engage us for advisory or fractional CFO services, we may receive financial records, business data and other information necessary to provide those services, under the terms of a separate engagement agreement.
  • Automatically collected information: when you visit our website, we may collect technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type and pages visited, through cookies and similar technologies. See our Cookie Policy for details.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Respond to enquiries and schedule consultations;
  • Provide, manage and improve our advisory and fractional CFO services;
  • Communicate with you about our services, including administrative updates;
  • Comply with legal, regulatory and contractual obligations;
  • Maintain the security and proper functioning of our website.

3. Legal Basis for Processing

Where applicable data protection law requires it, such as the UAE Personal Data Protection Law (Federal Decree-Law No. 45 of 2021) or the EU/UK General Data Protection Regulation, we rely on one or more of the following legal bases: your consent, the necessity of processing to perform a contract with you, our legitimate business interests, or compliance with a legal obligation.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information with:

  • Service providers who support our operations, such as email, scheduling and cloud storage providers, bound by confidentiality obligations;
  • Professional advisors, such as auditors or legal counsel, where necessary;
  • Regulators or authorities where required by law; and
  • A successor entity in the event of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets.

5. International Data Transfers

As we are based in Dubai, United Arab Emirates and work with clients worldwide, your information may be transferred to, stored, and processed in countries other than your own. Where required, we take reasonable steps to ensure such transfers are subject to appropriate safeguards.

6. Data Retention

We retain personal information for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes described in this Policy, including any legal, accounting or reporting requirements, after which it is securely deleted or anonymised.

7. Data Security

We apply reasonable administrative, technical and organisational measures designed to protect your information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have the right to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, to object to certain processing, or to request a copy of your data in a portable format. To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

Our services and website are directed at businesses and professionals. We do not knowingly collect personal information from individuals under the age of 18.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision. Material changes will be reflected on this page.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or how we handle your information, contact us at contact@consultingspero.info.
