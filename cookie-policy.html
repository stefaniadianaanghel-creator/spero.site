Cookie Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This Cookie Policy explains how Spero LLC FZ ("Spero", "we", "us" or "our") uses cookies and similar technologies on our website, and the choices available to you.

1. What Are Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites function properly, improve the browsing experience, and provide information to the website owner.

2. How We Use Cookies

We use cookies for the following purposes:

CategoryPurpose
EssentialNecessary for the website to function correctly, such as remembering your preferences during a session.
AnalyticsHelp us understand how visitors use our website, so we can improve content and navigation.
FunctionalRemember choices you make to provide a more personalised experience.
Third-partySet by services embedded on our website, such as scheduling or communication tools.

3. Third-Party Cookies

Some cookies on our website may be placed by third-party services we use to operate the site or support our business, such as web analytics or booking tools. These third parties may use cookies in accordance with their own privacy and cookie policies.

4. Managing Cookies

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings, including blocking or deleting cookies. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality of this website. You can find out more about managing cookies at aboutcookies.org or through your browser's help menu.

5. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time to reflect changes in the cookies we use or for operational, legal or regulatory reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
