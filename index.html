Questions

Frequently asked questions

How is a fractional CFO different from an accountant or bookkeeper?

An accountant records and reports what already happened. A fractional CFO uses those numbers to guide decisions ahead of time: pricing, hiring, cash runway, fundraising and where to invest or cut back.

How quickly can we get started?

Most engagements begin with a financial diagnostic, so we understand your numbers before proposing a scope of work. From there, we agree a start date that fits your reporting calendar.

Do you work with early-stage startups, or only established businesses?

Both. We work with founders building their financial foundation from day one, and with established businesses that need more rigor in their reporting and planning.
